# @keelwerk/module-members

Memberships, roles and invitations of one tenant. It builds on nothing at run time: `@keelwerk/module-accounts` and `@keelwerk/module-auth` are optional, and the module works beside either, both or neither.

- `members.membership` (history chain, data subject `subject`, unique per person and tenant): `role` (`owner`, `admin`, `member` or `viewer`, exactly one) and the `invitationId` it came from. Created by accepting an invitation only; the role history is the audit trail.
- Roles: the enricher `members.roles` (a fill of the core's `principalEnrichers`) gives a signed-in person of a tenant the role of their live membership as `roles` and as one membership of kind `members.membership`. It runs once per session and again at every revalidation, so a role change shows at the next one. `hasRole(ctx, ...roles)` (this package's own helper, for the rules of this module) reads the memberships, never `roles`, so another enricher or a test principal that names a role grants nothing here.
- Rules: every member reads the members; admins and the owner change a role (never to owner), remove a member and read and issue invitations; the owner alone moves ownership. A deny rule keeps the owner row out of `update` and `archive`: such a call answers `not_found`, as a revoked API key does. Ownership moves only through `members.membership.transferOwnership`, and the former owner becomes an admin.
- `members.invitation` (state history, data subject `subject`): `email` (sealed, found only by its keyed digest, unique among pending invitations), `role`, `status` (`pending`, `accepted`, `withdrawn`, `expired`), `invitedBy`, `publicId`, `tokenVerifier` and `expiresAt`. While pending the data subject is the invitation's own id; accepting moves it to the accepting person, so an erasure of that person reaches the invitation and its address reads as absent afterwards.
- `members.invitation.invite`: an admin or the owner invites an address as `admin`, `member` or `viewer`. An address with a pending, unexpired invitation is refused as a conflict that does not name it; an expired one is settled and replaced. The accept token `kwi_<public id>_<secret>` is shown once (`shownOnce`), only the verifier is stored, and neither the token nor the address reaches an event, receipt, job or log line.
- `members.invitation.createOwner`: bring-up. Only an operator may call it; a tenant with an owner refuses, and a repeat withdraws the earlier owner invitation.
- `members.invitation.accept`: a signed-in person presents the token. It works once and for seven days; every failure (unknown, wrong, used, withdrawn, expired) is the one answer `members.invitation_invalid`, and an unknown token costs one verification like a known one. The address is not compared with the person's. A person who is already a member, and a second owner, are refused.
- Withdrawing is the generated `update` of an invitation under the rule `members.invitation.withdraw`: only the inviter, only a pending invitation, only to `withdrawn`. The keyed timer `expire:<invitation id>` settles an expired invitation; `accept` checks the expiry itself, so a late timer lets nothing through.
- Delivery: the extension point `invitationDelivery` (a set) takes fillers that send the message. No filler ships in M2: without one `invite` and `createOwner` answer the token, which the caller hands on; with one the token goes to the filler only and the answer carries none.

`src/module.generated.ts` is produced by `pnpm manifests` and the files under `migrations/own/` by the CLI migrations producer; neither is edited by hand.
